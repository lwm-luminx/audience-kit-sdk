- **Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.2.1 (Intellectual Property)** says
  developers must not use protected third-party material, such as copyrighted
  works, without permission, and that apps "should be submitted by the person
  or legal entity that owns or has licensed the intellectual property." An app
  built from this code without a license from Love Wins Media, Inc. does not
  meet that guideline.

  property rights of others.

Love Wins Media, Inc. may report infringing apps to the app store concerned,
